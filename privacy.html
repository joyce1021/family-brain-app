Privacy policy

Last updated 10/24/2026.

family-brain is a private tool used by one household. Only members of that household connect their own Google accounts to it.

What it reads

With each person's permission, it reads their Gmail messages and Google Calendar events. It asks Google for read-only access.

What it does with it

It uses what it reads only to make the household's own to-do list, calendar reminders and daily summary.

It does not sell, share or send this data to advertisers, data brokers or anyone outside the household. It does not use it to train AI models.

Where it is kept

Access keys stay on the household's own computer. Full email text is not stored. Short summaries, such as dates and to-dos, are kept in the household's private notes.

How to stop it

Anyone who connected an account can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services

family-brain's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
